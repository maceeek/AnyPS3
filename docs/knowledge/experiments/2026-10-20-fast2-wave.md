**Derived:** the cloud round gives +31% FPS in the game; the GPU time per frame fell by ~18 ms, consistent with the MSAA plane

**Derived — VSYNC quantization:** in mode 0 the game flips on vblank (59.94 Hz), so the frame rate moves in steps; a median
of exactly 29.97 means most frames take two vblanks. 60 FPS needs every per-frame path (RSX worker CPU, GPU, main thread,
SPU jobs on the critical path) under 16.7 ms, with headroom ~13 ms. The RSX worker (22.9 ms) is the first limit; the main
thread (15.8 ms busy) is at the budget edge; the GPU (13 ms at low clocks) is inside it.

## Suite over 472 and the fp_lazy slowdown (16:10–16:45)

**Verified:** full suite over `source-snapshot-472` (`local/ratchet/suite-472/summary.json`): 813/813 tests executed, one error —

(zero, copy independence, move, fill) and mincore residency < 16 of 1024 pages after a PpuState is constructed; a mutated table that
zero-fills fails it (`resident<16`). Targeted modules OK: test_ppu_fp_lazy, test_memory, test_guest_memory_guard,
test_guest_memory_rsxmem, test_par1/2/10/11/13, test_ppufast_memory, test_reservation_fast_path, test_guest_heap.
